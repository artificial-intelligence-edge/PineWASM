TradingView® and Pine Script® are trademarks of TradingView, Inc. All
third-party trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. No
trademark license, endorsement, or other relationship with TradingView is
granted or implied by this repository.

## The PineWASM name

The composite name **PineWASM** identifies this independent project. “Pine”
refers to the Pine Script® source language and “WASM” refers to the
WebAssembly execution target. The name does not claim that PineWASM is
official Pine Script® software or that every Pine Script® feature is supported.
Compatibility is stated feature by feature in
[`docs/pine-v6-coverage.md`](pine-v6-coverage.md).

Capitalization, spacing, or a non-affiliation notice does not by itself prevent
possible confusion, so public descriptions must follow the rules below.

## References to third-party marks

References to Pine Script® and TradingView in this repository identify the
compatibility target or cite the public Pine Script® language reference as the
language specification. Keep those references descriptive and accurate:

- Use “Pine Script®” and “TradingView®” on first use in a public-facing
  document; plain text is acceptable afterward in the same document.
- Describe PineWASM as “independent” and “Pine Script®-compatible.” Do not call
  it “official,” “the Pine compiler,” “TradingView's compiler,” or an edition
  of Pine Script®.
- Scope compatibility claims to the versioned coverage matrix. Do not claim
  complete, perfect, drop-in, or one-to-one compatibility unless PineWASM's tests
  support that exact statement.
- Do not use TradingView logos, icons, or other trade dress as PineWASM
  branding.
- Do not imply that PineWASM accesses, redistributes, or includes TradingView
  market data. Hosts provide their own data.
- Do not direct PineWASM support requests, security reports, or bug reports to
  TradingView.

## Use of the PineWASM name

You may use the PineWASM name to refer truthfully to this project, its APIs,
or compatible artifacts. Do not use the name or any future project logo in a
way that implies publication, maintenance, certification, or endorsement by
AI Edge when none exists. Forks and modified distributions should use a name
that distinguishes them from the upstream PineWASM project.
